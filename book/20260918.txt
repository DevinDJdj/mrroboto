@@Who were the commanders of the Persians?

==
Based on the passage provided, the commanders of the Persians are *not named*.

However, the text does describe the composition of this large, threatening force:

 > "...for the *commanders of the Persians had joined together to form one single army* and were marching upon Miletos..."

The forces described as comprising this unified Persian army include:

 *   The *Kiliki* (Kilikians).

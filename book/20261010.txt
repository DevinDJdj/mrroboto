@@Describe the inking balls and the process in this printing.

==
Based on the passage, here is a description of the inking balls and the associated printing process:

 The Inking Balls:**
They were **large hemispherical balls** that were stuffed internally and covered with leather. They served as the primary tool used to apply ink during the printing process.

 **The Process (Old Method):**
1. **Ink Collection:** The printer would take a small portion of ink from an ink-block placed on these balls.
2. **Inking the Balls:** To ensure the ink was spread uniformly, the printer had to continually roll the balls against each other in various directions until a thin layer of ink was evenly distributed across their surface.
3. **Transferring Ink:** Finally, the type was inked by transferring this film of ink onto it using a "kind of rolling action."

The passage notes that this old method was inefficient because large quantities of ink often got stuck near the edges of the balls, becoming unusable waste (a thick black crust), and the amount of ink applied to the type could not be regulated precisely.

@@What is the process of whitening the pins?

==
Based on the text, the process of whitening the pins is described as follows:

1. **Preparation:** The tinned pins are placed into a boiler containing a solution of **tartar in water**.
2. **Immersion/Boiling:** They are also mixed with a quantity of **tin in small grains** (or tin granules).
3. **Duration:** This mixture is generally kept **boiling for about two hours and a half.**
4. **Washing:** After boiling, the pins are removed into a tub of water that has been thrown with some bran, specifically "for the purpose of washing off the acid liquor."
